# KringleCrate User Guide

Welcome to KringleCrate, the Secret Santa plugin for Minecraft! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to participate in the gift exchange.

## Joining the Event

To join the Secret Santa event, use the following command:

`/kc join`

This will opt you into the event and create your wishlist profile, allowing you to add items you'd like to receive.

## Your Wishlist

Once you've joined, you can manage your wishlist to let your Secret Santa know what you'd like!

*   **View your wishlist:** `/kc wishlist view`
*   **Add an item to your wishlist:** `/kc wishlist add <item>`
*   **Remove an item from your wishlist:** `/kc wishlist remove <item>`

## Finding Your Recipient

After the reveal date, you can find out who you'll be giving a gift to with this command:

`/kc reveal`

## Submitting a Gift

You can give two types of gifts: items and currency.

*   **To give an item:** Hold the item in your main hand and use the command: `/kc submit`
*   **To give currency:** Use the command: `/kc submit currency <amount>`

## Redeeming Your Gifts

Once the redemption period begins, you can receive all the gifts that have been given to you. This includes both items and any currency that has been sent your way.

Use the following command to redeem your gifts:

`/kc redeem`
